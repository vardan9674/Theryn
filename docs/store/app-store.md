- **Bundle ID:** com.theryn.app · **SKU:** theryn-ios
- **Primary category:** Health & Fitness · **Secondary:** Sports
- **Privacy policy URL:** https://www.theryn.fit/privacy
- **Support URL:** https://www.theryn.fit (add a support page or the contact email)
- **Age rating questionnaire:** no violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs,
  gambling, contests or unrestricted web access. "Medical or treatment
  information": none. User-generated content: messages between a coach and
  their own clients only (no public profiles or feeds). Expected rating: 4+.

  is set, so no export documentation is needed.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label")

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Yes.
**Tracking:** No data is used to track.

| Data type | Collected | Linked to user | Tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact info → Name | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| Contact info → Email address | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| Health & fitness → Health (weight, height, measurements) | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| Health & fitness → Fitness (workouts, sets, plans) | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| Financial info → Other financial info (fees a coach records) | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| User content → Other user content (messages, notes, reports) | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| Identifiers → User ID | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |
| Identifiers → Device ID (push token) | Yes | Yes | No | App functionality |

Not collected: location, contacts, photos or videos, audio, browsing or search
history, purchases, usage data, diagnostics, advertising data, sensitive info,
HealthKit data.

## Review notes (paste into "App Review Information")

> clients. Sign in with Apple or Google. After signing in, choose "I'm an

> (coaches). It deletes the account and all its data immediately.

> Coach "payments" are notes of money clients pay their coach outside the app.
